# AutoClick 1.6.4

## Improvements

- **A cleaner control pod.** The floating hand, the expanded control panel and the long-press
  hide popup now share one dark teal glass look with light icons and clear state colours
  (teal = running / play, red = stop). It reads as a deliberate remote control over any app,
  in light or dark theme, instead of a washed-out grey plate.
- **A smaller hand.** The floating hand is now 44 dp (was 56 dp) with a proportionally smaller
  glyph, label and countdown ring — it is a controller, so it takes less of your screen.
- The control panel is slightly narrower with tighter buttons to match the compact hand.

Everything else behaves exactly as in 1.6.3: tap = panel, drag = move, long-press = hide popup,
Stop retracts the panel, and the settings page brings a hidden hand back.

## Install

Download `AutoClick-1.6.4.apk` below and sideload it. Accessibility ("AutoClick taps") and
"Display over other apps" must be enabled; the app walks you through both.
