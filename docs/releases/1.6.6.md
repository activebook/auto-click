# AutoClick 1.6.6

## Fixes & Polish

- **Screen-on resume without a lock screen.** Devices without a keyguard now resume a paused run cleanly when the screen wakes back up, instead of waiting indefinitely for a keyguard-unlock broadcast.
- **Reliable hand visibility & live notification updates.** Preserved your floating-hand preference across cold start and permission toggles, and refreshed the ongoing notification whenever the interval changes or the hand attaches.
- **Exact-spot crosshair & overlay stability.** Crosshair flashes now always resolve against the exact inset-aware screen area used by the tap engine, and overlay animations, coroutines, and long-press gestures cancel/guard cleanly across rotations and teardowns.
- **UI & dark-mode polish.** Added keyboard **Done** submission on interval and tap-budget inputs, fixed singular `1 tap` summary wording, matched the cold-start window background in dark mode, and routed denied/older-API notification toggles to system settings.

## Install

Download `AutoClick-1.6.6.apk` below and sideload it. Accessibility ("AutoClick taps") and "Display over other apps" must be enabled; the app walks you through both.
