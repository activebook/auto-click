# AutoClick 1.6.7

## Fixes & Improvements

- **Instant floating hand on permission grant.** Enabling **Accessibility ("AutoClick taps")**—whether manually in Android Settings or via Android's Accessibility Shortcut Button—or **Display over other apps** now shows the floating hand immediately without returning to AutoClick, even if it was previously hidden.
- **Visible right over Accessibility Settings.** Once Accessibility connects, the floating hand attaches via `TYPE_ACCESSIBILITY_OVERLAY` so Android's Settings screen no longer hides it while you toggle the switch, and tears down cleanly when revoked.
- **Immediate hide on permission revoke.** Disabling Accessibility or revoking **Display over other apps** immediately hides the floating hand and stops any active run.

## Install

Download `AutoClick-1.6.7.apk` below and sideload it. Accessibility ("AutoClick taps") and "Display over other apps" must be enabled; the app walks you through both.
