# AutoClick 1.6.3

## Improvements

- **Hide the hand with a long-press.** Hold the floating hand for a moment (~0.4 s) and a small
  eye-off button pops up beside it. Tap the eye to hide the hand and stop the run; tap anywhere
  else to dismiss the popup. This replaces the `Hide hand` row that used to sit in the panel's
  settings page — one gesture instead of three taps.
- Dragging or tapping the hand still behaves exactly as before; the hold is only recognised when
  the finger stays still, so it never steals a drag or a tap.
- While the popup is open, tapping is paused-safe: no tap is dispatched into the popup itself.

Unchanged from 1.6.2: the `Show floating hand` switch in the app settings page brings the hand back,
the notification keeps a `Hide hand` action, and pressing Start re-shows the hand automatically.

## Install

Download `AutoClick-1.6.3.apk` below and sideload it. Accessibility ("AutoClick taps") and
"Display over other apps" must be enabled; the app walks you through both.
